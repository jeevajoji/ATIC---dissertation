ATIC causal-DSAD screening dataset
==================================

Purpose
-------
This is a small, sequence-disjoint internal screen for deciding whether DSAD
has enough signal to justify paper-scale experiments. It is not itself a
publication benchmark or a SOTA experiment.

Frozen split
------------
Train:      Bosphorus, HoneyBee, YachtRide
Validation: ShakeNDry
Test:       Beauty (locked)

Bosphorus, HoneyBee and YachtRide have already influenced development.
ShakeNDry is used for every DSAD decision. Beauty must not be previewed,
evaluated, or used for model, beta, epoch, lambda, or checkpoint selection.

Preparation
-----------
Run from the repository root on CSML01:

  python -m atic.uvg_prep \
    --source-root "$HOME/datasets" \
    --accept-uvg-by-nc

The command:

1. Reuses the existing UVG_bosphorous, UVG_Honeybee and UVG_yachtride sources.
   Duplicate aliases such as 00001.png and frame_00001.png are verified by
   decoded pixels and collapsed to their single source-frame index.
2. Downloads the official 1920x1080 8-bit Beauty and ShakeNDry archives.
3. Verifies exact raw frame counts and byte sizes.
4. Samples fixed zero-based frames 12 + 25*k.
5. Writes five non-overlapping 512x512 RGB crops per selected frame.
6. Creates and verifies immutable train/validation/test manifests.

Expected counts are 360 train crops, 60 validation crops and 120 locked test
crops. The generated paths are:

  $HOME/datasets/UVG_DSAD_screen_v1/dataset_512
  $HOME/datasets/UVG_DSAD_screen_v1/frozen_split

UVG is distributed under Creative Commons BY-NC for non-commercial use:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/

Screening rule
--------------
Run matched NoDSAD/DSAD experiments on validation only. Advance only when the
validation BD-rate is consistently favourable across three paired seeds and
the gain is materially larger than pilot noise. Do not pass --evaluate-test
until all choices and the analysis plan are frozen.

Immediate beta calibration
--------------------------
The two-epoch pilot used beta 0.05. Its weighted DSAD term was only about
0.0042 percent of the rate-distortion objective, so that pilot tested wiring
rather than useful distillation strength.

Run the one-command calibration:

  CUDA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID \
  CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,1 \
  CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG=:4096:8 \
  PYTHONHASHSEED=42 \
  python -m atic.dsad_screen

This runs beta 0.5, 2 and 8 against a fresh same-GPU beta-zero control at
lambda 0.0067, seed 42, batch size 4 and 12 epochs. Two isolated processes use
physical GPUs 0 and 1. This means six model trainings in total; GPU 0 runs two
complete pairs and GPU 1 runs one. Every pair uses actual validation
bitstreams. The runner refuses a modified or stale repository, a wrong frozen
bundle, a missing GPU, silent CPU fallback, or test evaluation.

This is calibration, not evidence for the paper claim. Select one beta only
after checking actual validation rate-distortion, DSAD alignment and gain-map
saturation. Then freeze it and confirm with seeds 42, 123 and 999 before a
multi-lambda study.

Scientific boundary
-------------------
These crops come from only five sequences in the same UVG capture family.
They are temporally correlated and cannot support a broad generalisation or
SOTA claim. Paper evidence still needs a large independent image-training
corpus, full-resolution Kodak/Tecnick/CLIC evaluation, six RD points, at
least three paired seeds, confidence intervals, actual bitstreams and ideally
a second backbone. The colour-conversion provenance of the three existing PNG
sources is also unknown; final paper work should regenerate all sources from
official raw YUV through one recorded conversion pipeline.
